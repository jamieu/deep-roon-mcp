- Every `image` key now comes with an `image_url`, ready to load from your Core (and `images` with `image_urls`). Roon has two kinds of image keys that aren't interchangeable: Deep Roon's internal ones (short, like `doicaaaa`) are served at `/image/<key>.jpg` (or scaled, `/image/<key>.300.jpg`), the official API's (long hex, in `roon_now_playing`) at `/api/image/<key>`.
