TIDAL, next to Qobuz — whatever is signed in on your Roon Core.

- **Search** finds TIDAL too, and every album and track now says where it's from: `library`, `Qobuz` or `TIDAL` (a streaming album in your library keeps its service). Before, everything streaming was called Qobuz
- **`roon_new`** has TIDAL's shelves with `service: "tidal"`: new, recommended, top, rising and masters — albums, or tracks — and TIDAL's genre pages
- **`roon_genres`** gives TIDAL's genres and moods
- **New: `roon_tidal_playlists`** — TIDAL's editorial playlists (new, recommended, exclusive, masters, popular …), by genre or mood, and your own TIDAL mixes (My Daily Discovery, My Mix, My Most Listened …). Read them with `roon_playlist`, play them with `roon_play_playlist`
- **`roon_artist`** and **`roon_favorites`** can ask TIDAL instead of Qobuz
- Setup for the **ChatGPT desktop app** and Codex in the README

Fifty-one tools now. Unofficial — use it entirely at your own risk; see the README.
