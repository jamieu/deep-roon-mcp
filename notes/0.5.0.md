- **Read-only library** — a new setting (Claude Desktop › Settings › Extensions › Deep Roon MCP, or `DEEP_ROON_READ_ONLY=library`): Deep Roon never changes your Roon library. The six tools that write into the Core — playlists, hearts, Listen Later, adding to the library, Live Radio favourites and DSP — aren't there at all; playing and controlling zones still work. `DEEP_ROON_READ_ONLY=all` goes further: Deep Roon only looks (no playing, queueing or zone changes either)
- README: a word of care before the tools that change your library — they write through Roon's internal protocol, which Roon Labs doesn't support for others. Keep a recent backup of your Roon database before you let an assistant change things
