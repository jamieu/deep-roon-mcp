- Album types: every album now says what it is — `album_type` is main, single, ep, compilation, soundtrack, show or other, as in Roon's Focus (Album Type). Filter on it in `roon_library` and `roon_focus` with `album_type` ("ep", or "single, ep") or leave types out with `exclude_album_type` ("single, ep" for main releases only), and `roon_focus` counts the albums per type. Thanks to the tester who asked
- Bootlegs: `bootleg: true` or `false` filters on Roon's bootleg flag
- Tip: after an update, restart your MCP client (or refresh its tool list) — a client that keeps the old tool list won't know the new options
