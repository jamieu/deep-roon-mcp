- The whole play history, not just the recent part. `roon_history` used to return the newest 500 plays at most and `roon_top` counted the newest 20,000 — with a history since 2015 that stopped somewhere in 2024. Now:
  - `roon_history` takes `before` (YYYY-MM-DD) and `offset`: "what did I play in March 2019" asks Roon for the plays before 1 April 2019 directly, however far back.
  - `roon_top` counts the whole history; with `since` Roon itself returns only the plays from that date (HistoryQueryCriteria::MinPlayDate), so a period is fast.
  - A very long history is read in pages of 500 within a time budget; if it doesn't fit, the answer says how far back it got instead of quietly stopping. Thanks to the forum tester who noticed
